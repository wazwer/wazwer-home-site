The Review!
The Piña Colada; nemesis of bartenders everywhere. I love them, and despite being made of only 4 ingredients (pineapple, coconut, rum, ice) - they are weirdly variable!

I think this is due to a few factors:
  • Is the pineapple juice fresh or from concentrate?
  • Was coconut cream used or cream of coconut? coconut milk?
  • What kind of rum was used; dark, light?
  • Ice ratio?
  • Was anything additional added?
Supposedly, a "true" pina colada uses specifically "Coco Lopez" Cream of Coconut. Personally I don't think any one company should own a recipe. That said, this stuff is delicious, unlike regular Coconut Cream (which is not sweet) it's 50% sugar cane, so it's more akin to condensed milk. That will either be a pro or a con to you depending on your personal taste. Of course, you could just add sugar to regular coconut cream to get the same effect.


The current can label seems to have been designed at some point in the 1990s (?) and it is a thing of beauty. It perfectly captures the tacky vintage charm of the Piña Colada, just look at that mascot. Anyway, on with the reviews!
