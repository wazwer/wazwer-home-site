Tips & Tricks

Clip Studio Paint
Here are some cool things Clip Studio can do that Photoshop can't (that I use a lot!): - Ribbon brushes - limited color palette - Lasso select across multiple layers and move that selection - Fill line art that has gaps
Photoshop Krita - Comes with a shortcut to flip the canvas horizontally by default and it doesn't lag horrifically like photoshop does when it does it.
