Sonic SatAM

Unfortunately I am terminally Sonic-brained. I've had the sonic bug since I was about 8 and I've just come to accept that it's here to stay. Anyway, about SatAM:
Urkle sonic may not be my favourite sonic (though still iconic), but the environment art for robotropolis lives rent free in my head. The overall vibe and tone of this show changed my life (the writing less so haha). I wish we still got localizations of media that varied so much from the source material like this, it gives franchises mystique and variety.

Vocaloid

Call me basic, but I love Hatsune Miku. I love her color palette (the original silver and teal) and I love the idea of an AI vocalist from the future. I love the fact that she's a blank slate you can repurpose to fill any role in any genre. I just think she has an all around 10/10 character design. Also, MMD! What would the world be without MMD. I spent many all-nighters in highschool trying to figure out half-width-katakana-only bowlroll passwords. My long time favourite producer is PinnochioP because he's also having a perpetual existential crisis.
Teto is also pretty cool, and I'm glad she's finally getting the recognition she deserves.
