🎲 DICE is an ethereal trickster kangaroo with quantum powers 🎲

 Generally unpredictable. You're never quite sure if they're being serious or funny. Likes to mess with people. Fascinated by death. Very theatrical and chaotic but also philosophical and existential. May or may not be real.

 "Ah, snake eyes. Pity. I had such a finale planned."
 "You landed on double zero! That means I get to improvise~"
 To a vending machine: "Speak your price, ancient oracle of snacks."
 Gesturing at rat: "It told me; you should bet on heads rather than tails this time."

 Tall! e.g., 7 or 8 ft
 Doesn't have a fixed gender, can appear as whatever they like. Feel free to draw more masculine or more feminine!
 Please do have fun with their holographic fur pattern!! It can be anything!

 Inspired by a lot of characters including but not limited to: Q/Guinan from Star Trek, and Nights (into Dreams), among others.

 (I'm working on a 3D model but I don't know if I'll ever finish it 😅)
