It’s _Kascade!!!! My techno hacker dork. She doesn’t have much in the way of powers, but she’s techy and can get around fast. She can be snarky, but usually not in an overtly mean way. She’s impulsive and something of a kleptomaniac.

> “So I hotwired the matrix to explode. That means we have roughly 8 minutes to get out of here – race ya?”
> “You’re thinking linearly. I’m thinking panicked inspiration.”
> “I tuned the gauntlet again. No, I didn’t ask anyone.”
> “Ooh, shiny. Probably cursed. Definitely mine.”
> About her sentient bike: “You don’t steer her – just hold on and hope she likes you today.”

She doesn’t have a fixed species but I like to think of her as a Saluki that’s been gene spliced with wolf and ant DNA. She’s got lots of cyborg/robot bits because that’s my jam lol.

If you want to give her clothes then she would probably vibe with formal military jackets, grungy leather jackets, anything with shoulder pads and any kind of sports gear.

She is roughly between 5 and 6 feet not including antenna.
