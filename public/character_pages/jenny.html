J.N.E.

Meet J.N.E. (or "Jenny" as she's known) she might look cute, but she's actually a battle hardened mercenary. Her head unit can be detached and placed inside different mech suits.
She leads excursion teams out into the brainframe, sometimes she is the only one who returns. She is stoic and cold the majority of the time but can snap in violent ways.
Her mech bodies are owned by the corporation tasked with maintaining the brainframe network.
Her view is that organic bodies are weak and inefficient. She keeps her organic head, due to being superstitious about machine intelligence.
"Full 'droids are a waste of time. Too predictable."
Some people fear her, some people revere her.
Species: Stoat
