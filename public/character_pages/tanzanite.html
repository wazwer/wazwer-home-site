Tanzanite is a mean girl with a bite to back up her bark. She was born into a wealthy family that operates a gemstone cartel. Her brother; Obsidian, was set to inherit most of the power, but since his betrayal she is now thrust into a leadership role she never really considered preparing for. She is highly manipulative and surprisingly technical - despite her air headedness. Often planting spy gadgets on people. Her loud personality has garnered her something of a reputation, especially online, which is a point of contention amongst her family who prefer to stay in the shadows.

Species: Lop-eared Rabbit
